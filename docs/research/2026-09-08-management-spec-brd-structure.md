# Management SPEC-001 business requirements structure

Date: 2026-09-08. Evidence class: external standards/professional guidance and editorial recommendation. This note creates no product requirements, approvals or conformity claims.

## Selected basis and limitations

[ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148:2018](https://www.iso.org/standard/72089.html), edition 2, addresses requirements processes, information items, their content and presentation. The official catalogue reports confirmation in 2024 and a successor under development. The [official public preview](https://www.iso.org/obp/ui?_escaped_fragment_=iso%3Astd%3Aiso-iec-ieee%3A29148%3Aed-2%3Av1%3Aen) distinguishes a business requirements specification (BRS, 3.1.4) from a software requirements specification (SRS, 3.1.27), and lists BRS guidance at 8.2. The full normative body of 8.2 was not accessed; this work does not reproduce its outline or claim conformance.

[IIBA The Business Analysis Standard v2.0](https://www.iiba.org/globalassets/business-analysis-resources/the-business-analysis-standard/files/the-business-analysis-standard.pdf), copyright 2025, supports applying guidance to the initiative's context (1.1). Its [public section 4.4](https://www.iiba.org/knowledgehub/the-business-analysis-standard/4-implementing-business-analysis/4-4-understanding-requirements-and-designs/) distinguishes business, stakeholder, solution and transition requirements and discusses tracing needs through subsequent work. This supports a business-level management brief linked to the detailed requirements, not a replacement technical authority.

The [IIBA public glossary](https://www.iiba.org/career-resources/a-business-analysis-professionals-foundation-for-success/babok/glossary/) distinguishes business rules, acceptance criteria, assumptions and constraints. [Official requirements-analysis task cards](https://www.iiba.org/contentassets/be4de45e850d4d30b78e8c4d7ca0c967/all-task-cards-req-analysis-and-design-def.pdf), v2.0, distinguish verifying clarity/consistency and validating alignment with business needs. The [IIBA-published quality checklist](https://www.iiba.org/contentassets/1dc6a35dff8c46ff85c6cb6b916b7ea3/9-quality-criteria-for-requirements.pdf), contributed by George Sioutzos, copyright 2021, is an editorial aid rather than normative ISO text.

## Tailored four-section recommendation

Four content sections and six total pages are an IDEA editorial choice, not an ISO or IIBA requirement. Actual product rules must come from the IDEA sources below.

| Management section | Content and intended decision | Guidance applied |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Business objectives and scope | Intended outcomes, stakeholder needs, included work, exclusions and operating context | IIBA requirement classification; ISO public BRS definition |
| 2. Document-management requirements | Business obligations, responsible actors, meaningful conditions and exceptions | IIBA business-rule glossary and quality guidance |
| 3. Review and release process | Product lifecycle, decision responsibility, released scope, required outputs and preserved history | IIBA business-rule/change concepts; IDEA alone defines product workflow semantics |
| 4. Acceptance and proposed approval | Observable acceptance criteria, provisional limits, unresolved inputs and exact decision scope | IIBA acceptance/assumption/constraint definitions and verification/validation cards |

The [IIBA approval card](https://www.iiba.org/contentassets/49f563f67a294abbaae89384bf9e2db5/task-card-approve-req-and-designs.pdf) and [change-assessment card](https://www.iiba.org/contentassets/148b69ad11e24a43aafeb9b4cd192299/task-card-assess-req-and-designs-changes.pdf) support recording agreement and assessing change impacts. They do not define IDEA's Release state, independent approver rule or generation semantics.

## IDEA source boundary

Use SPEC-001 0.13 and FEATURE-001 0.11, with DOC-03 0.4, DOC-04/06 0.9, DOC-08 0.5 and VVP 0.10. The Human four-page Word document is a style reference whose compression is being superseded only in a separately named management rendition. Preserve the originals. The new rendition does not modify these requirements or claim approval of unresolved inputs.

Preserve business meaning when shortening: changed versus unchanged Check-in, release of the confirmed hold after success, local-work preservation on rejection, new Revision after Release, atomic confirmed scope, independent approval, source-specific BOM/PDF, configurable policies and reproducible history. Do not introduce test results, measured performance or broader format support from this editorial work.

Research was performed against official ISO and IIBA pages/PDFs. No paid ISO/BABOK text was acquired. Sources inform structure and writing; missing IDEA operating thresholds and business decisions remain open.
