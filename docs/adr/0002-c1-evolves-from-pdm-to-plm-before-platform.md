# C1 evolves from PDM to PLM; Platform D waits for C2

IDEA Engineering C1 owns the complete PDM semantics and expands into PLM inside the same Platform Capability Unit. Interoperability Fabric D is designed only after at least one independent C2 creates concrete cross-application needs; this keeps C1 independently deployable and avoids a premature distributed platform while requiring stable public identity and contract discipline from the beginning.
