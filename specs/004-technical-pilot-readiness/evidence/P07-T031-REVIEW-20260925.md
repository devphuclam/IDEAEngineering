boundary able to support multiple Vault locations later. The reviewer accepted the other items
in the presented checklist review. CHK016 was already reviewer-marked. CHK030 remains unchecked
because CHK033 supersedes its incomplete `PG4 PASS`-only wording; it is not counted as a current

Document, Generation or Artifact identity. The F05 review must check that this boundary remains
visible in code/configuration and is not replaced by a hard-coded one-path assumption. This is an
implementation-conformance check against the [environment contract](../environment-profile.md#42-ubuntu-development-and-later-deployment-contract)
and the [approved design scope](../../../docs/product/instances/idea-engineering/registers/CHG-2026-09-25-pg2-pg3-approval.md),
not an additional PH1 feature. A second Vault, replication, repair, failover, durability and
multi-GB performance remain outside F01–F05 and require their own later evidence and gate decision.
